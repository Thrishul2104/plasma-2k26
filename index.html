About PLASMA

PLASMA 2K26 is the flagship technical fest of Jawaharlal Nehru New College of Engineering (JNNCE), Shivamogga. Step through the gate, take on technical challenges, pitch your ideas, and compete for cash prizes alongside students from across the country.

About The College

Jawaharlal Nehru New College of Engineering (JNNCE), Shivamogga, is an engineering institution affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi. PLASMA 2K26 is hosted on its campus on Savalanga Road, Navule, bringing together students, coordinators and faculty from across departments to run the fest.
